Our story

We started ATLAS because rental gear kept failing us.

Three friends, one blown-out tent zipper on a Karakoram trek, and a shared decision to build gear that doesn't quit halfway up the mountain.

Gear tested where it's used

Every product spends at least one full season in the hands of our test group — trail guides, porters, and weekend hikers alike — before it's approved for sale. We'd rather delay a launch than ship something we haven't carried ourselves.

That's slower than most outdoor brands are comfortable with. It's also why our return rate is under 2%.

Built to be repaired, not replaced

Every pack, tent, and jacket comes with a lifetime repair promise. Blow a zipper on year six? Send it in — we'll fix it or replace the part, no receipt required.

It's a simple bet: gear that lasts is better for the person carrying it and better for the trail it's carried on.

What we hold ourselves to

Fit

Designed for real bodies

Adjustable frames and four size ranges, fitted on actual hikers — not a single sample body.

Materials

Sourced, not assumed

We name our fabric mills and factories on every product page. No black-box supply chain.

Impact

Carbon-neutral by default

Every shipment offset automatically. No opt-in checkbox, no extra charge.
