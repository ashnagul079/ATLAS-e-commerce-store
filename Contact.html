Common questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the country, and 7-12 days for international orders. Free on orders over $75.

What's covered under the lifetime repair promise?

Zippers, seams, straps, and buckles on any product we've sold, for as long as you own it. Send it in and we'll assess it at no charge.

Can I return an item after using it on a trip?

Yes — our 30-day trail trial means you can actually use the gear outdoors and still return it if it's not right for you.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to over 30 countries. Shipping cost and delivery time are calculated at checkout based on your address.
